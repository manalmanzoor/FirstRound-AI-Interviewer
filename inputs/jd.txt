Junior AI Engineer
Northwind Labs — Karachi, Pakistan (Hybrid)
Experience: 0–2 years

ABOUT NORTHWIND LABS

Northwind Labs builds applied AI products for logistics and customer
operations teams across South Asia. Our small engineering team ships
fast, favors working software over long design docs, and expects every
engineer to own their feature end-to-end — from prototype to production.

ROLE OVERVIEW

We're hiring a Junior AI Engineer to help build and ship LLM-powered
features across our product line: retrieval-augmented chat, workflow
automation agents, and voice/conversational interfaces. You'll work
closely with senior engineers on real production systems from day one,
not a sandboxed side project.

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Design, build, and iterate on LLM-powered features (RAG pipelines,
  agents, structured extraction) from prototype through production.
- Integrate LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or open-source models)
  into existing FastAPI/Python backends and React frontends.
- Write evaluation scripts and test cases for AI features — we care
  about measuring quality, not just shipping demos.
- Collaborate with design/product on human-in-the-loop UX for agentic
  features: confidence signaling, source grounding, approve/edit/reject
  flows.
- Debug and improve prompt reliability, structured-output parsing, and
  latency in production LLM calls.
- Contribute to a full-stack codebase as needed (Python backend, React
  frontend, basic mobile familiarity a plus).

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

- 0–2 years of professional or substantial project experience building
  software, with hands-on exposure to LLM/AI application development
  (internship, freelance, coursework, or personal projects all count).
- Solid Python fundamentals; comfortable reading and writing FastAPI
  or similar backend code.
- Practical experience with at least one of: RAG systems, LLM agent
  frameworks (LangGraph, LangChain, or similar), vector databases, or
  voice/conversational AI.
- Familiarity with modern frontend development (React or similar) —
  we don't expect deep frontend expertise, but you should be able to
  wire a UI to an API.
- Comfortable with Git/GitHub workflows and reading others' code.
- Strong written communication — you'll document tradeoffs and explain
  technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders.

NICE TO HAVE

- Experience designing human-in-the-loop or trust/transparency UX for
  AI features (confidence indicators, citation grounding, approve/edit
  flows).
- Exposure to multi-modal AI (text + image/table/chart reasoning).
- Familiarity with Firebase, vector databases, or other lightweight
  data infrastructure.
- A portfolio of shipped side projects — we weight demonstrated
  initiative heavily for junior roles.

WHAT WE OFFER

- Direct mentorship from senior AI/ML engineers.
- Ownership of real features from week one, not a rotation program.
- Competitive junior-level compensation for the Karachi market.
- Hybrid work with flexible hours.
